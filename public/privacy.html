Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Stream Command is a prototype for organizing streaming services, saved titles, and phone-to-TV commands. This notice explains what the current app stores and what happens when you connect Trakt.

Information stored by Stream Command

The app stores saved titles and selected services in this browser so they remain available after a refresh. If you sign in to a Stream Command account, the app also saves your watchlist and selected services in Firebase Firestore to restore them for that account. TV pairing uses a pairing code and session details in Firebase.

Trakt connection and watchlist sync

Connecting Trakt is optional. Sign-in and permission are handled by Trakt; Stream Command does not ask you to enter your Trakt password into this app. When authorized, the app requests your Trakt movie and show watchlists and imports title metadata (title, media type, and an identifier) into your Stream Command watchlist. It calls Trakt's API from your browser and does not send your Trakt tokens to Stream Command's Firebase database.

For this prototype, the Trakt access and refresh tokens are stored in this browser's local storage on the device where you connected. Anyone with access to that browser profile may be able to use the stored session. Do not connect on a shared or untrusted device. A browser reset or cleared site data may require you to connect again.

Use Disconnect Trakt in My Watchlist to stop future syncs and remove the locally stored Trakt tokens. Disconnecting does not automatically erase titles already imported into Stream Command. Use a title's Remove control to hide it from future Trakt syncs on this browser; the title remains in your Trakt account. The Restore hidden titles control clears those local exclusions so those items can be imported again. If you are signed in to Stream Command, the updated watchlist is synchronized to your Firebase account.

Provider credentials

Stream Command does not collect your Netflix, Prime Video, Tubi, or other streaming-service passwords. The browser-extension importer is a testing tool, not a requirement for normal app use. Direct synchronization from individual streaming providers is not offered unless a supported integration is implemented.

Service providers

Firebase provides Stream Command account authentication, Firestore storage, and TV pairing. When you connect Trakt, Trakt processes authorization and API requests under its own privacy terms. Streaming availability lookups may be sent to the configured metadata service when you request "Where to watch." Stream Command's hosting provider also receives standard web-request information needed to deliver the site.

Retention and choices

Watchlist data remains in browser storage and, when applicable, your Firebase account while it remains in your saved list. Disconnecting Trakt removes the locally stored Trakt tokens but leaves imported titles in your saved list. Removing a title changes the Stream Command watchlist, not the watchlist in your Trakt account.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the prototype's features and storage practices change. A dedicated privacy-contact email has not yet been configured for this project; do not submit sensitive information through the app.
